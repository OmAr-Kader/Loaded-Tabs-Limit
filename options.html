Loaded Tabs Limit

Keeps the most recently used tabs loaded and discards the rest.

General

Never discarded: the active tab of each window, tabs playing audio, tabs marked non-discardable in brave://discards, and filtered sites. Audible, filtered, non-discardable and (if protected) pinned tabs don't use one of the N slots. A tab that stops playing audio becomes a candidate again.

Filtered sites

    github.com filters that site and all its subdomains, every page. github.com/OmAr-Kader filters only that exact page (any ?query and #hash are ignored). http://, https:// and www. are ignored.
